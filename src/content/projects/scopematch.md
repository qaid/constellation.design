### Background & Overview

If you're a quality engineer and you need to find a testing lab accredited to run a specific test on your product, you have a problem. There's no single place to look. Every European country maintains its own accreditation database. Germany has DAkkS, the UK has UKAS, France has COFRAC, the Netherlands has RvA, Italy has ACCREDIA, Spain has ENAC. Six countries, six databases, six interfaces, six languages. The same test method has different reference numbers in each country. Finding the right lab means checking them all, one at a time.

I built ScopeMatch to fix that.

#### The real challenge is taxonomy, not technology.

The interesting problem here isn't building a search engine. It's normalization. The same tensile test might be referenced as "EN ISO 6892-1" in Germany, "BS EN ISO 6892-1" in the UK, and "NF EN ISO 6892-1" in France. Multiply that across thousands of test methods and six languages, and you have a classification problem that's genuinely hard to solve at scale.

I built a data platform that aggregates lab profiles and scope data from each national accreditation body and normalizes everything into a unified taxonomy. AI-powered classification maps test method variants across borders, so a search for one finds them all. The result: 13,700+ labs with over 122,000 capability variants, all searchable from one interface.

#### Strategy as product.

ScopeMatch isn't a side project; it's a business. The directory is the first phase of a larger platform, with the product strategy designed to expand from data aggregation into services that connect labs with the teams who need them.

The initial six countries - Germany, the UK, France, the Netherlands, Italy, and Spain - were chosen deliberately. Together they cover the majority of testing demand on the continent, with more markets planned.

### The Outcome

ScopeMatch is live at [scopematch.eu](https://scopematch.eu) with 13,700+ labs across 6 countries, 97.5% geocoded, generating 10,000+ indexable pages. The platform serves quality engineers, procurement teams, and lab managers looking for accredited testing partners across European borders.
